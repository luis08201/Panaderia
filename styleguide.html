Heading levels / H1–H6

Headings organize your page like chapters in a book. H1 is the main title; H2 introduces a section; H3–H6 organize smaller parts inside it. Use the levels in order. The examples show their usual sizes on your screen.

Make this guide your own

Start with the shared palette and type settings in css/tokens.css. The examples use the same component styles as the bakery. Update the brand name and logo lockups here when you personalize the template.

Swatch values and spacing labels read the shared settings automatically. The sample card and form copy are illustrative; they are not connected to the product catalog or an inbox.
